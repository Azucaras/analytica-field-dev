### El Niño, strong-to-very-strong through January, March 2027
**Sequence.** The ONI record `(read, https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/data/indices/oni.ascii.txt)` shows: DJF 2024-25 **−0.46**, MAM 2025 **+0.02** (neutral), SON 2025 **−0.57**, OND 2025 **−0.61**, NDJ 2025-26 **−0.60**, a weak La Niña, then DJF 2026 **−0.39**, JFM 2026 **−0.21**, FMA 2026 **+0.11**, MAM 2026 **+0.46**, AMJ 2026 **+0.95**, MJJ **+1.39**, JJA **+1.80**, JAS **+2.16**, ASO 2026 **+2.46**.

**Numbers.** CPC discussion of **8 October 2026** `(read)`: El Niño Advisory; SST anomalies exceeded **+4.0 °C** in parts of the eastern equatorial Pacific; Niño-3.4 **+2.1 °C**, Niño-3 **+3.0 °C**, Niño-1+2 **+3.9 °C**; subsurface anomalies above **+10.0 °C**; SOI significantly negative. Three-month RONI ≥ +2.5 °C probabilities were **54 %** for SON, **83 %** for OND and **70 %** for NDJ. Separately, CPC's forecast assigned **greater than 83 %** probability to strong-to-very-strong El Niño conditions through JFM 2027; this is a different forecast quantity and threshold `(read)`. Next discussion **2026-11-12**.

**What ends it.** ENSO-neutral re-establishment, which CPC does not project before February to April 2027. Nothing a grower does ends it; it ends itself. Operationally, the thing that ends its relevance is the February to April 2027 transition, which is exactly when Brazilian safrinha corn and US 2027 planting decisions are being made.

### US growing-season drought footprint
**Sequence and numbers.** NCEI July 2026 drought report `(read)`: over the five weeks **2026-06-30 → 2026-08-04**, D1 to D4 went **47.8 % → 48.5 %** of CONUS; D2 to D4 **30.7 % → 28.6 %**; D3 to D4 **10.9 % → 9.5 %**; D4 steady at **1.0 %**; D0 to D4 **67.0 % → 71.0 %**. A persistent ridge over the western and central US drove record-to-near-record heat across the Northern Plains, Upper Midwest and Intermountain West; the eastern half got above-average rain from Gulf/Atlantic moisture.

**Reading.** The aggregate number understates the problem: total drought area grew while severe drought shrank, meaning drought spread into new, previously-fine country rather than deepening where it already was. That is the pattern that forces rotation change on farms that had no history of it.

**What ends it.** The report window ends with the 2026-08-04 observation; this pack does not assert a later CONUS drought condition. Locally, measured rainfall, soil moisture and any irrigation allocation changes would determine whether the acreage response remains relevant.
