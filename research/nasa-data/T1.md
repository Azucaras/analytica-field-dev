# T1 NASA dataset capability pack

Scope: NASA datasets relevant to crop-rotation decision support in Brazil and the USA. This pack covers SMAP, ECOSTRESS, GPM IMERG, HLS, MODIS, GRACE/GRACE-FO, SRTM/NASADEM, GEDI and NASA POWER. All current-state checks are dated 2026-11-10 unless noted; product documentation and validation sources may be older and are dated at point of use.

Labels: `(read)` = opened the source and read the relevant text; `(URL verified, not read)` = URL resolved, claim taken from snippet/abstract/metadata; `(discovery only)` = seen cited, not opened; `(inference)` = this pack's reasoning from the read facts, not a source statement. All CMR queries were run 2026-11-10.

## Per dataset

### SPL3SMP_E — SMAP Enhanced L3 daily 9 km soil moisture (V006)

**What the instrument physically measures.** The SMAP L-band radiometer (1.41 GHz) measures naturally emitted microwave brightness temperature; soil moisture is retrieved from the dielectric sensitivity of that emission, and the product is a daily composite of half-orbit L2 retrievals posted to 9 km EASE-Grid 2.0 (read, user guide Dec 2023 and ATBD Rev G Oct 2021). The radar that was to supply 10 km information failed in July 2015, so every "enhanced" SMAP soil-moisture product is radiometer-only (read, JPL instrument page).

**Proxy physics and where it breaks.** Emission depth is the top ~5 cm, and the retrieval assumes the tau-omega model with ancillary vegetation water content and soil temperature (read, ATBD §2). It breaks when vegetation water content exceeds 5 kg/m2, when the ground is frozen or snow-covered, when the footprint contains open water, steep topography or urban fraction, and when RFI contaminates L-band (read, user guide §2.5 and ATBD L1 requirement). It also breaks as a resolution claim: 9 km is interpolation of a ~36 km footprint, so two adjacent 9 km cells are not independent measurements (read, SPL2SMP_E user guide §1.3.3).

**Depth and footprint.** 0-5 cm vertical support; ~36 km native footprint, 40 km radiometer requirement, 9 km grid posting (read, ATBD Table 1 and user guide Table 5).

**What a farmer would have to believe.** That a ~36 km area average (roughly 100,000 ha) is a meaningful stand-in for his field; that moisture in the top 5 cm tracks the layer his crop roots in; and that a morning overpass value is representative of the day (read, ATBD on 6:00 a.m. descending-node choice).

**Two most credible validation / readme statements.**
1. NSIDC DAAC, SPL3SMP_E User Guide, Version 6, December 2023 — states 9 km grid, daily composite, VWC and frozen-ground limits, the 2019 and 2022 safe-mode gaps: https://nsidc.org/sites/default/files/documents/user-guide/spl3smp_e-v006-userguide.pdf (read, 2023-12).
2. O'Neill et al., 2019-08-15, "Calibration and Validation for the L2/3_SM_P Version 6 and L2/3_SM_P_E Version 3 Data Products", SMAP Project JPL D-56297 — reports ubRMSE 0.038 m3/m3 (SCA-V) and 0.036 m3/m3 against the 0.040 m3/m3 mission requirement: https://nsidc.org/sites/default/files/spl23a3smp3a_e_assessment20report20version206205b20195d.pdf (read, 2019-08-15).

### SPL2SMP_E — SMAP Enhanced L2 half-orbit 9 km soil moisture (V006)

**What the instrument physically measures.** The same L-band radiometer brightness temperatures, retrieved per half orbit and posted to 9 km; it is the parent product that SPL3SMP_E composites (read, SPL3SMP_E user guide §2.3).

**Proxy physics and where it breaks.** Identical physics and identical failure conditions to SPL3SMP_E. The extra failure mode at L2 is that per-granule quality flags are exposed rather than absorbed into a daily composite: frozen ground, precipitation, urban fraction and retrieval-success bits each gate the value (read, user guide §2.5.3 and flag table). The 9 km posting is explicitly described as interpolation from the ~36 km footprint (read, §1.3.3).

**Depth and footprint.** 0-5 cm; ~36 km native; 9 km grid; ~49-minute granule (read, CMR temporal resolution and user guide).

**What a farmer would have to believe.** That a single overpass at ~6:00 a.m. or ~6:00 p.m. local solar time represents the wetting state of his field, and that the retrieval flag he is ignoring does not apply to his cover, slope or irrigation method (inference from read flag definitions).

**Two most credible validation / readme statements.**
1. NSIDC DAAC, SPL2SMP_E User Guide, Version 6, December 2023 — the clearest official statement that 36 km native data are interpolated to 9 km: https://nsidc.org/sites/default/files/documents/user-guide/spl2smp_e-v006-userguide.pdf (read, 2023-12).
2. O'Neill et al., 2019-08-15, JPL D-56297 L2/3_SM_P v6 and L2/3_SM_P_E v3 assessment — core validation site and sparse-network metrics against the 0.040 m3/m3 requirement: https://nsidc.org/sites/default/files/spl23a3smp3a_e_assessment20report20version206205b20195d.pdf (read, 2019-08-15).

### SPL4SMGP — SMAP L4 3-hourly 9 km surface and root-zone soil moisture (V008)

**What the instrument physically measures.** Nothing directly. It is an assimilation product: 36 km SMAP brightness temperatures (descending and ascending passes, ~6:00 a.m. and ~6:00 p.m. local solar time) are assimilated by an ensemble Kalman filter into the NASA Catchment land surface model, and the output is 3-hourly 9 km fields (read, user guide Apr 2025 and IGARSS 2026 paper).

**Proxy physics and where it breaks.** The "measurement" is a model state constrained by microwave observations every 12 hours at 36 km. Root zone (0-100 cm) is never observed by SMAP; it is model physics plus precipitation forcing. It breaks where precipitation forcing is wrong (IMERG replaced the gauge product outside North America in V8, and IMERG version changes forced a full reprocess), where the land model's soil texture or depth assumptions are wrong, and where SMAP data are absent — the guide states L4_SM is available during the 2019 and 2022 SMAP gaps "albeit based primarily on the modeling system and not on concomitant SMAP observations" (read). ET, net radiation and soil temperature in the same file are labelled "additional research products (not validated)" (read).

**Depth and footprint.** Surface 0-5 cm vertical average; root zone 0-100 cm vertical average; 9 km grid; 3-hourly (read, user guide §1.2 and §1.5.4).

**What a farmer would have to believe.** That a land-surface model run at 9 km with satellite precipitation has useful skill at his field, and that a modelled 0-100 cm layer average represents the depth his rotation's roots actually occupy (inference from read layer definition).

**Two most credible validation / readme statements.**
1. NSIDC DAAC, SMAP L4 user guide, Version 8, April 2025 — layer definitions, 3-hourly cadence, availability through SMAP outages: https://nsidc.org/sites/default/files/documents/user-guide/multi_spl4smau-v008-userguide.pdf (read, 2025-04).
2. Reichle et al., "Version 8 of the NASA SMAP Level 4 Soil Moisture Product", IGARSS 2026 (NTRS 20260006721) — ubRMSE 0.039 m3/m3 surface and 0.026 m3/m3 root zone against core validation sites, ~2.5 day mean latency, and the statement that core-site measurements are insufficient for validation after 2021: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20260006721/downloads/IGARSS2026_Reichle_et_al_v3_eDAA.pdf (read, 2026-08 conference paper).

### TELLUS_GRAC-GRFO_MASCON_CRI_GRID_RL06.3_V4 — JPL GRACE/GRACE-FO mascon water storage

**What the instrument physically measures.** Time-variable gravity. Two satellites ~200 km apart in an 89-degree orbit range to each other at K-band; the changing inter-satellite range rate measures how Earth's gravity field changes from month to month, which is inverted for surface mass change and expressed as equivalent water thickness in cm (read, GRACE L3 handbook Table 2; PODAAC dataset page).

**Proxy physics and where it breaks.** Gravity change is total mass change: soil moisture, snow, surface water, groundwater and ice all sum together, and the product only reports the anomaly relative to the 2004.0-2009.999 mean, never absolute storage (read, JPL Tellus page and FAQ). Separating groundwater from total storage requires subtracting a land model, and the gain factors provided are tuned to seasonal near-surface water, so they bias groundwater estimates when the groundwater pattern differs (read, Tellus FAQ). Signal leakage and damping at 3-degree mascon scale are the core error, addressed but not removed by the CRI filter and by Wiese et al. (2016) scaling (read, Tellus page; scaling paper citation confirmed via Crossref, not read). GIA is removed with ICE6G-D and C20/C30 replaced with SLR solutions, which are corrections a user inherits silently (read).

**Depth and footprint.** No depth: it integrates the whole column including groundwater. Native footprint is a 3-degree equal-area spherical cap mascon, 4,551 of them, ~300-330 km; the 0.5 degree grid is oversampled posting (read, Tellus page and handbook).

**What a farmer would have to believe.** That a ~300 km average of total water storage, including aquifers he does not pump and snow he never sees, is a proxy for the water his rotation depends on (inference from read resolution and FAQ statements).

### ECO_L3T_JET — ECOSTRESS tiled ET
- **Physical measurement:** ECOSTRESS measures thermal-infrared radiance; JET is derived from LST, GEOS-5 meteorology/downscaled soil moisture and HLS vegetation/albedo, combined across four ET models (read, ATBD 2025-10-08: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ECOSTRESS-Collection-3/ECOv003-L3T-L4T-JET/main/documentation/ECOv003_L3_L4_JET_ATBD.md).
- **Proxy physics and break point:** ET is a modelled energy/water flux, not observed water use. Reanalysis soil moisture, clouds, advection and mixed pixels can break the estimate; “daily” ET integrates sunrise-to-sunset rather than a full continuous day (read, ATBD and FAQ).
- **Depth/footprint:** 70 m tiled output, derived from ~69 × 38 m thermal IFOV; no soil penetration depth (read, ATBD).
- **Farmer assumption:** One irregular clear-day estimate and model partition of soil evaporation/transpiration represent the target field and relevant fortnight (inference from read cadence/inputs).
- **Two credible readme/validation sources:** JET ATBD, 2025-10-08 (read), https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ECOSTRESS-Collection-3/ECOv003-L3T-L4T-JET/main/documentation/ECOv003_L3_L4_JET_ATBD.md; Hu et al. 2023, Water Resources Research (abstract read), https://doi.org/10.1029/2022WR034132.

### ECO_L4T_ESI — ECOSTRESS tiled evaporative stress index
- **Physical measurement:** Derived actual/potential ET ratio from PT-JPL-SM model chain, not a direct measurement (read, JET ATBD/catalog).
- **Proxy physics and break point:** An instantaneous ratio may flag stress but is not a standardized multi-week climatological anomaly; high stress can be overestimated (read, Thakur et al. 2026 abstract). The cited validation concerns non-tiled ESI products, not direct validation of this tiled V003 dataset (read).
- **Depth/footprint:** 70 m output, same modeled footprint as JET; no soil depth (read).
- **Farmer assumption:** A one-pass ratio is useful to trigger scouting, not to compare seasonal resilience (inference from read acquisition cadence and product definition).
- **Two credible readme/validation sources:** JET ATBD, 2025-10-08 (read), https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ECOSTRESS-Collection-3/ECOv003-L3T-L4T-JET/main/documentation/ECOv003_L3_L4_JET_ATBD.md; Thakur et al. 2026 (abstract read), https://doi.org/10.1080/07038992.2026.2645742.

### ECO_L2T_LSTE — ECOSTRESS LST and emissivity
- **Physical measurement:** Five thermal-infrared bands measure emitted radiance at approximately 8–12.5 µm; TES retrieves surface temperature and emissivity (read, ATBD: https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/2235/ECO2_LSTE_ATBD_V1.1.pdf).
- **Proxy physics and break point:** Temperature/emissivity separation is ill-posed and relies on emissivity assumptions; clouds and thin cirrus can corrupt apparent temperature. Retrieved skin temperature is not air, canopy water use or root-zone temperature (read, ATBD/FAQ).
- **Depth/footprint:** 70 m grid; native IFOV ~69 × 38 m; radiometric surface skin (read, ATBD).
- **Farmer assumption:** A 70 m pixel at a drifting overpass time represents the management unit, and cloud screening removed thin cloud (inference from read footprint/FAQ).
- **Two credible readme/validation sources:** LSTE ATBD V1.1, 2023-10-25 (read), https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/2235/ECO2_LSTE_ATBD_V1.1.pdf; Hulley et al., IEEE TGRS (abstract read), https://doi.org/10.1109/TGRS.2021.3079879.

### GPM_3IMERGDF — IMERG Final daily precipitation
- **Physical measurement:** Constellation microwave precipitation estimates are calibrated to GPM DPR, motion-vector interpolated and filled with IR estimates between microwave observations; Final additionally uses monthly GPCC gauge adjustment (read, V07 ATBD: https://arthurhou.pps.eosdis.nasa.gov/Documents/IMERG_V07_ATBD_final.pdf).
- **Proxy physics and break point:** Satellite retrieval estimates precipitation over a grid cell rather than measuring a farm rain gauge. Skill weakens over frozen surfaces, complex terrain, coasts and warm-rain regimes; changing constellation inputs can affect long-term trends (read, ATBD).
- **Depth/footprint:** Surface precipitation rate over ~0.1° (~11 km at equator), accumulated into daily totals (read, ATBD).
- **Farmer assumption:** Area-average rain, adjusted using much coarser gauge information, represents a particular field (inference from read grid/gauge support).
- **Two credible validation/readme sources:** IMERG V07 ATBD, 2023-07-12 (read), https://arthurhou.pps.eosdis.nasa.gov/Documents/IMERG_V07_ATBD_final.pdf; Sungmin et al. 2017, HESS gauge comparison (abstract read), https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-21-6559-2017.

### GPM_3IMERGDL — IMERG Late daily precipitation
- **Physical measurement:** Same constellation microwave/IR precipitation estimate as Final, without Final's later monthly gauge adjustment (read, ATBD: https://arthurhou.pps.eosdis.nasa.gov/Documents/IMERG_V07_ATBD_final.pdf).
- **Proxy physics and break point:** Area-average estimate can miss warm rain, coastal and orographic events; official latency differs, 12 h in ATBD versus 14 h in GES DISC page (read).
- **Depth/footprint:** Approximately 11 km cell; daily aggregate of half-hour intervals (read, ATBD).
- **Farmer assumption:** Next-day regional rain context suffices only as a planting-window clue and is locally checked (inference from read resolution and latency).
- **Two credible readme/validation sources:** IMERG V07 ATBD, 2023-07-12 (read), https://arthurhou.pps.eosdis.nasa.gov/Documents/IMERG_V07_ATBD_final.pdf; Sungmin et al. 2017 (abstract read), https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-21-6559-2017.

### HLSL30 — harmonized Landsat optical reflectance
- **Physical measurement:** Landsat OLI measures reflected visible, NIR and SWIR solar radiation; HLS provides harmonized surface reflectance, NBAR and cloud/shadow/snow QA (read, HLS User Guide v2 April 2026: https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/1698/HLS_User_Guide_V2.pdf).
- **Proxy physics and break point:** Reflectance responds to chlorophyll absorption, leaf/canopy scattering, exposed soil and residue; it is not yield, species or biomass by itself. Aerosols, bright surfaces, clouds/shadows, snow and changing subpixel mixture break interpretation (read, guide/known-issues docs).
- **Depth/footprint:** 30 m optical surface pixel, no penetration depth (read, guide).
- **Farmer assumption:** A locally validated classifier distinguishes cover crop from weeds/fallow and enough clear scenes fall in the relevant window (inference from read reflectance/cadence).
- **Two credible readme/validation sources:** HLS User Guide v2, updated 2026-04 (read), https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/1698/HLS_User_Guide_V2.pdf; L30 Known Issues, 2026-04 (read), https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/2434/HLS_v2.0_L30_known_issues_April2026.pdf.

### HLSS30 — harmonized Sentinel-2 optical reflectance
- **Physical measurement:** Sentinel-2 MSI measures reflected visible, NIR and SWIR solar radiation; HLS harmonizes reflectance, NBAR and cloud/shadow/snow QA and resamples native 10/20/60 m bands to its 30 m grid (read, HLS User Guide v2 April 2026: https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/1698/HLS_User_Guide_V2.pdf).
- **Proxy physics and break point:** Reflectance responds to vegetation pigment/canopy and soil/residue mixture, not crop identity or yield. Aerosols, bright targets, cloud/shadow, snow and harmonization/geolocation errors limit interpretation (read, guide and S30 known-issues doc).
- **Depth/footprint:** 30 m output pixel, no penetration depth; native bands have mixed 10/20/60 m resolution (read, guide).
- **Farmer assumption:** A locally validated classifier separates cover crops from weeds/fallow and clear scenes capture establishment/termination windows (inference from read measurement and cadence).
- **Two credible readme/validation sources:** HLS User Guide v2, updated 2026-04 (read), https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/1698/HLS_User_Guide_V2.pdf; S30 Known Issues, 2026-04 (read), https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/2435/HLS_v2.0_S30_known_issues_April2026.pdf.

### MOD13Q1 — MODIS vegetation indices
- **Physical measurement:** Red/NIR reflectance is converted to NDVI/EVI; a best-quality observation in each 16-day window is selected using quality and viewing geometry (read, guide/ATBD: https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/621/MOD13_User_Guide_V61.pdf; https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/104/MOD13_ATBD.pdf).
- **Proxy physics and break point:** NDVI responds to red absorption/NIR scattering but saturates at high LAI; EVI's aerosol adjustment has blue-band sensitivity. Indices do not identify crop species or stress cause (read, ATBD/guide).
- **Depth/footprint:** 250 m pixel (~6.25 ha at nadir); canopy-integrated optical signal, no depth (read).
- **Farmer assumption:** A mixed 250 m composite represents the field and the selected greenest observation is representative of the interval (inference from read method).
- **Two credible readme/validation sources:** MOD13 User Guide v6.1, 2019-09 (read), https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/621/MOD13_User_Guide_V61.pdf; MOD13 ATBD v3 (read), https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/104/MOD13_ATBD.pdf.

### MOD11A1 — MODIS land surface temperature
- **Physical measurement:** Terra MODIS thermal bands 31 and 32 measure emitted radiance; split-window algorithm retrieves day/night LST and emissivity (read, guide/ATBD: https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/715/MOD11_User_Guide_V61.pdf; https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/119/MOD11_ATBD.pdf).
- **Proxy physics and break point:** Retrieval depends on emissivity, water vapour and clear-sky classification. It measures mixed land-cover radiometric skin temperature, not 2 m air, seed-zone or canopy temperature (read).
- **Depth/footprint:** 1 km at nadir, surface skin (read, guide/ATBD).
- **Farmer assumption:** A 1 km daily overpass reflects field heat/frost conditions despite mixed pixels and changing Terra overpass time (inference from read resolution/mission status).
- **Two credible readme/validation sources:** MOD11 User Guide v6.1, 2019-06 (read), https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/715/MOD11_User_Guide_V61.pdf; MOD11 ATBD v3.3 (read), https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/119/MOD11_ATBD.pdf.

### SRTMGL1 — SRTM terrain elevation
- **Physical measurement:** Single-pass C-band interferometric radar phase from the 2000 Shuttle Radar Topography Mission is converted to elevation (read, SRTM guide: https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/179/SRTM_User_Guide_V3.pdf).
- **Proxy physics and break point:** Interferometric phase follows the scattering volume's phase centre; under forest it can lie within canopy rather than bare ground. Shadow, layover, voids and flattened water bodies limit terrain inference (read, guides).
- **Depth/footprint:** 1 arc-second (~30 m) posting; radar scattering depth varies by surface and vegetation, not a soil profile (read).
- **Farmer assumption:** 2000 terrain still represents a field that may since have been terraced, levelled or drained (inference from read epoch).
- **Two credible readme/validation sources:** SRTM User Guide v3, revised 2015-10 (read), https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/179/SRTM_User_Guide_V3.pdf; Farr et al. 2007, Reviews of Geophysics (URL verified, not read), https://doi.org/10.1029/2005RG000183.

### GEDI L4B — gridded aboveground biomass
- **Physical measurement:** GEDI 1064 nm full-waveform lidar measures canopy/ground return structure from ISS; L4 biomass is modelled from footprint estimates and gridded by statistical inference (read, guide/ATBD: https://data.ornldaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/public/gedi/GEDI_L4B_Gridded_Biomass_V2_1/comp/GEDI_L4B_Gridded_Biomass_V2_1.pdf; https://data.ornldaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/public/gedi/GEDI_L4B_Gridded_Biomass_V2_1/comp/GEDI_L4B_ATBD_V2.0.pdf).
- **Proxy physics and break point:** Biomass is predicted from waveform metrics using plot-calibrated models; sparse tracks and model transfer mean a 1 km grid is not wall-to-wall observation. Dense canopy, clouds, slopes and inadequate track intersections affect sampling (read, ATBD/guide).
- **Depth/footprint:** ~25 m lidar footprints sampled along tracks; published L4B grid 1 km. It does not measure belowground biomass or soil carbon (read).
- **Farmer assumption:** Forest-calibrated biomass model and 2019–2023 sampling transfer to the land-cover type and field being screened (inference from read model/time span).
- **Two credible readme/validation sources:** GEDI L4B User Guide v2.1 revision 2023-10-29 (read), https://data.ornldaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/public/gedi/GEDI_L4B_Gridded_Biomass_V2_1/comp/GEDI_L4B_Gridded_Biomass_V2_1.pdf; GEDI L4B ATBD v2.0 (read), https://data.ornldaac.earthdata.nasa.gov/public/gedi/GEDI_L4B_Gridded_Biomass_V2_1/comp/GEDI_L4B_ATBD_V2.0.pdf.

### NASA POWER daily point API
- **Physical measurement:** POWER is not a single instrument measurement; meteorology is provided by global reanalysis/model systems and solar radiation from satellite-based radiation products (read, methodology: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/).
- **Proxy physics and break point:** A grid-cell model/reanalysis value is not a farm sensor. Elevation/terrain mismatch and modeled soil-water variables limit field-level inference (read, POWER FAQ and hydrology docs).
- **Depth/footprint:** Daily request samples ~0.5° grid; meteorology at 2 m and hydrology states are not depth-resolved field measurements. Documentation conflicts on the hydrology grid width (read).
- **Farmer assumption:** Broad climate normals bound rotation feasibility, not actual field water balance (inference from read grid/methodology).
- **Two credible readme sources:** POWER daily API docs (read), https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/daily/; POWER latency FAQ (read), https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/faqs/data/; supporting accuracy methodology (read), https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/sources_meteorology/.

## Cannot do

- No dataset here establishes that a rotation caused higher yield, profit, soil carbon, soil health or lower input costs; none provides a counterfactual experiment (inference from measurement scope).
- No product independently identifies full rotation history, crop species/cultivar and management without locally validated classification and ground truth; vegetation reflectance is not crop identity (read, HLS/MODIS docs).
- SMAP does not resolve a farm field: 9 km posting derives from ~36 km native radiometer footprint; SMAP L4 root-zone moisture is modelled, not measured (read, SMAP docs).
- GRACE cannot attribute basin storage change to a rotation or isolate groundwater without modeling other mass components; native support is ~300–330 km (read, JPL handbook/Tellus FAQ).
- ECOSTRESS cannot provide continuous daily ET/stress history for one field: overpasses are irregular, cloud-limited, and JET daily output is daytime-only (read, FAQ/ATBD).
- IMERG is not a field rain gauge; ~11 km grid is coarse. V07 Final record stops 2025-09-30 (read, ATBD/GPM transition notice).
- HLS/MODIS cannot establish yield, belowground root disease or soil organic carbon; the optical signal samples canopy/surface reflectance (read, guides).
- MOD13Q1/MOD11A1 cannot resolve most small fields. Terra operations were scheduled to end 2027-02, with no morning-orbit successor identified in transition material (read, NASA Terra/LAADS sources).
- SRTM cannot show erosion, drainage alteration or land levelling after the 2000 mission; GEDI L4B cannot establish annual-crop biomass or soil-carbon change (read, product docs).
- POWER cannot say what happened at one farm on a specific day; it is gridded model/reanalysis context, not local rain-gauge or probe data (read, methodology).
