## Problem

Block 6 answers "what happened on this polygon" with numbers that carry their source, basis, date and resolution. A producer reading those numbers still cannot act on them, because the screen offers no comparison between the rotations on the table, no way to ask what a drier season would do, no calendar that says when each crop may be planted and on whose authority, and no way to enter the field or the practice the producer already knows. Block 5 selects a polygon, and a polygon is somebody else's field boundary, not the producer's.

Block 7 adds the decision and the hand that enters what only the producer knows. The trap it is written against is the one that already bit this project once: a form whose controls had no consumer, whose values persisted nowhere, and whose unknown default hid a measured value. Every control in this block therefore names its consumer, its unit, its storage path and the criterion that fails when the consumer is gone, and the one field that cannot meet that bar is dropped rather than shipped.

The second trap is the ranking itself. Four weighted criteria over four scenarios is small enough to look authoritative and has no calibrated truth behind it, so the spec makes the formula printable, the scores visible with their basis, the missing inputs named, and the abstention honest: with the fixture as it stands the comparator prints no rank at all, because three of its four criteria have no score. That abstention is the correct state, not a defect, and the criterion that measures the reordering uses injected scores the way block 1 measures the `modelled` class with an injected row.

## Users and scope

- Users: the producer who weighs water, income, residue and nitrogen before choosing the next sequence, and the judge who checks that a weight can reorder a list but can never relabel a measurement.

### The comparator with producer weights (F-15)

- R-11 (ubiquitous): The comparator SHALL rank the rotation scenarios of one field on exactly four criteria, in this fixed order: `water`, `income`, `residue`, `nitrogen`. The ids are data values of Table A, the labels are catalog keys, and no fifth criterion and no per field criterion set SHALL exist.
- R-12 (ubiquitous): Each criterion SHALL carry one integer weight from 0 to 100 in steps of 5, default 25, four weights at once per field. A weight SHALL be changed only by its own slider or by its own arrow key step, SHALL be stored per field as R-53 describes, and SHALL NOT be derived from a score, a basis or a fixture value. Zero is a legal weight and means the criterion is not part of the decision.
- R-13 (ubiquitous): The weighted total of a scenario SHALL be `total = sum over the scored criteria of (w_c * n_c) / sum over the scored criteria of w_c`, rounded to one decimal with round half up, where `w_c` is the weight of criterion `c` and `n_c` is that scenario's score on `c`. Because every score is an index on a 0 to 100 scale by R-18, no rescaling exists: the total is the weighted mean over the criteria that have a score, it stays on the same 0 to 100 scale, and the numerator and the denominator are printed so a reader can recompute it. A score outside 0 to 100 SHALL be rejected at entry with the exact text of Table E and SHALL NOT be clamped.
- R-14 (ubiquitous): Eligibility and coverage SHALL be computed per scenario. The eligible set is every criterion whose weight is above zero. The coverage of a scenario is `round(100 * sum of the weights of its eligible criteria that have a score / sum of the weights of its eligible criteria)`, an integer with round half up. A scenario is rankable when its coverage is at or above 80. When the eligible set is empty because every weight is zero, the ranking card SHALL print `Every weight is zero` and no total and no rank SHALL print for any scenario.
- R-15 (state-driven): While a scenario is not rankable, its four scores SHALL still print with their basis and date, its total SHALL print as `not ranked`, no rank number SHALL print for it, and the card SHALL print the abstention text naming every eligible criterion that has no score for that scenario. The threshold of 80 percent is the block 1 R-07 coverage rule carried to this surface, and it applies per scenario and not per field.
- R-16 (state-driven): While fewer than two scenarios are rankable, the ranking card SHALL print the abstention text `Fewer than two scenarios are scored on enough criteria to rank`, every scenario SHALL print no rank number and no total, and every scenario card SHALL stay visible with its scores. A single rankable scenario SHALL NOT print its total as a ranking number in that state.
- R-17 (ubiquitous): Rankable scenarios SHALL be ordered by descending total. Two totals that round to the same one decimal value SHALL share the lowest of their rank numbers and SHALL be printed with the mark `tie`; the ranks SHALL be competition ranks, so three rankable scenarios with two equal totals read 1, 1, 3. Scenarios that share a total SHALL be ordered by their index in the scenarios array the fixture or the response returns, ascending, and no other tie breaker SHALL exist. Not rankable scenarios SHALL print after every rankable one, in array order.
- R-18 (ubiquitous): Every scenario and criterion SHALL carry either a score row or nothing. A score row SHALL carry `value` (an integer from 0 to 100), `basis` (one of `observed`, `retrieved`, `modelled`, `entered`), `source`, `as_of` and `edited_at`. A score has exactly two producers: the producer declaration, whose `basis` is `entered` and whose `source` is the producer, and the derivation rules of R-19, whose `basis` is `modelled` because a computed index is not a measurement of the field, with `source` naming the derivation and the declared inputs it read. A criterion with neither SHALL be absent from the scenario's scores and SHALL print the abstention text of R-15, never a zero and never a default.
- R-19 (ubiquitous): The derivations SHALL be exactly these two and no others. `water` SHALL be the block 6 metric `rain_percentile` of the field, an integer from 0 to 100, with the basis, source, as_of and resolution of that row: it is a field level number and is shared by every scenario of the field. `income` SHALL be `round(100 * min over the scored scenarios of break_even / this scenario's break_even)` where `break_even` is the declared cost per hectare of the scenario divided by the declared price of its last crop, so the scenario with the lowest break even scores 100 and every other scores below it. `residue` and `nitrogen` SHALL have no derivation, because this repository holds no residue, cover fraction or soil nitrogen product, and block 6's soil carbon and MapBiomas classes are not residue; both are declared only.
- R-20 (ubiquitous): Changing a weight, a score or a declaration SHALL reorder scenarios and SHALL change the printed total, and SHALL NOT change the `basis`, `value`, `as_of` or `resolution` of any evidence row or provenance row that the card displays. This carries block 1 R-15 to this surface: no control of this block sets, clears or overrides the label of a row, and no label is derived by matching a substring of a metric id, a source string or a scenario name.
- R-21 (ubiquitous): The ranking card SHALL print the formula with the numbers substituted, in the form `total = (w1 * n1 + w2 * n2 + ...) / (w1 + w2 + ...) = value`, for every scenario, and SHALL print, once per field, the statement that the water score is one field level number and therefore moves every scenario the same way, so a change to the water weight alone cannot change the order.

### The condition simulator (F-16)

- R-22 (ubiquitous): The simulator SHALL offer exactly two controls: `Rain change`, an integer percentage of decline from 0 to 30 in steps of 5, default 0, and `Temperature change`, a number of degrees Celsius from 0 to 3 in steps of 0.5, default 0. No other transformation, crop coefficient or season offset SHALL exist.
- R-23 (ubiquitous): The simulator SHALL apply the transformation to the block 6 climate series exactly as returned, with `rain' = rain * (1 - decline / 100)` applied to every valid `PRECTOTCORR` day and `tmax' = tmax + delta`, `tmin' = tmin + delta` applied to every valid temperature day, filter the `-999` fill value before the arithmetic exactly as block 6 R-17 does, and recompute every aggregate with the pure functions block 6 exports (`src/lib/aggregates.ts` and `src/lib/water-balance.ts`). Block 7 SHALL NOT contain a second implementation of an aggregate formula.
- R-24 (state-driven): The card SHALL print the measured value and the simulated value of every aggregate side by side, and for each number that is not recomputed it SHALL print the measured value with the mark `unchanged` and the reason. `rain_total`, `dry_spell_days`, `gdd10` and `tmin_min` SHALL be recomputed. `frost_days` SHALL NOT be recomputed when the temperature change is above zero, because POWER's `FROST_DAYS` is a flag the daily series does not derive, and the card SHALL print that reason. `gwetroot_mean`, `rain_median`, `rain_percentile` and `hot_days` SHALL NOT be recomputed, because they are measured, historical or comparative numbers that the declared transformation does not define, and the card SHALL print that reason.
- R-25 (state-driven): The simulated water balance SHALL print `in` as the simulated sum of rain, `out` as the measured block 6 `EVLAND` sum marked `unchanged`, and `remaining` as the simulated `in` minus that unchanged `out`, with the statement that no crop water demand model exists in this repository (block 6 R-24 and OD-02), so the simulated `remaining` is an upper bound and not a balance a crop would experience. When block 6's `out` is absent, all three simulated balance numbers SHALL abstain.
- R-26 (ubiquitous): Every simulated number SHALL carry the mark `simulated`, the card heading SHALL print `Scenario, not a forecast` at every width and in every state, and the provenance of a simulated number SHALL print the source of the input series, that series' `as_of`, the transformation as a string such as `rain -20 percent`, and the instant of the computation. A simulated number SHALL NOT be presented as measured, and no simulated number SHALL enter a comparator score, a ranking total or a stored declaration.
- R-27 (state-driven): While block 6's climate section is `abstained` or `unavailable`, the simulator SHALL abstain with the reason block 6 returned, SHALL print no simulated number, and SHALL NOT substitute a fixture series, a normals year or a neighbouring field. The simulator SHALL NOT mutate the block 6 payload, and its `Reset` control SHALL return both controls to zero and the card to its measured-only state.

### The planting window calendar (F-17)

- R-28 (ubiquitous): The calendar SHALL read the same window source that block 4 R-31 names for the active region, through the block 4 route `GET /fields/{field_id}/planting-windows`, and for the Brazil source that is the snapshot `artifacts/zarc-mt-snapshot.json`, sha256 `b53c8a7be60948fccb72e5cfa108b95debfdd932f5f15b315363d42a332dfde9`. It SHALL apply the same season key normalisation (`Y/YY` read as `Y/YYYY`), the same JSON key `window_risk_20`, and the same envelope rule block 4 R-31 fixes: for each crop and season, the earliest `start_date` and the latest `end_date` over the rows of that crop and season.
- R-29 (ubiquitous): The calendar SHALL print one row per crop and per season with the window start, the window end, and for each of the two limits its own source: the `Portaria` number and date of the rows the envelope is built from, the risk level `20` as a percentage, the `Cod_Solo` values and the `Cod_Ciclo` and cultivar cycle labels the envelope covers, and the number of rows enveloped. - R-30 (ubiquitous): The calendar SHALL carry, per crop, the block 6 numbers that bear on the window as separate annotated values, each with its own basis label, source and date: the longest dry spell, the frost days and the rain percentile of the chosen date's window. - R-32 (state-driven): The calendar SHALL print the abstention texts of block 4 R-31 unchanged for a municipality, a crop or a season the source does not hold. - R-33 (ubiquitous): The calendar SHALL show the windows of the season derived from the chosen date only, using block 4's `seasonForDate`, and SHALL NOT show the windows of the other season the snapshot holds.
- R-34 (ubiquitous): For the same field and the same chosen date, the four envelopes the calendar prints SHALL equal the four windows block 4 R-31 draws on the band, value for value. Neither surface SHALL compute an envelope with a different rule, and a disagreement between them SHALL fail this requirement.

### Field registration (F-21)

- R-35 (ubiquitous): A field SHALL be registered by exactly three paths: drawing its boundary in the boundary editor of R-36, importing a GeoJSON file, and importing a KML file. No other path SHALL create a field, and no field SHALL be created from a weather station, a municipality, a centroid buffer or a fixture record.
- R-36 (ubiquitous): The boundary editor SHALL be a map inset inside the `Field record` surface, mounted from `src/components/BoundaryEditor.tsx`, drawing the active region's basemap through the block 4 adapter and the block 5 polygon layer read only. A press on the inset SHALL add one vertex; `Undo` SHALL remove the last vertex; `Close ring` SHALL close the ring and run the validation of R-38; `Cancel` SHALL discard the ring and leave no field. The editor SHALL require at least three distinct vertices, SHALL show the running area in hectares beside the ring, and SHALL be the only place where a ring is created by hand.
- R-37 (ubiquitous): The GeoJSON reader SHALL accept a `Feature` whose `geometry.type` is `Polygon`, a bare geometry object whose `type` is `Polygon`, and a `FeatureCollection` holding exactly one polygon feature; it SHALL read the first ring as the outer ring and SHALL reject a polygon with holes. The KML reader SHALL accept a KML 2.2 document and read `<Polygon>` with `<outerBoundaryIs><LinearRing><coordinates>`, parsing `lon,lat` pairs separated by whitespace and ignoring an altitude if present; it SHALL reject a document with more than one polygon and a polygon with an `<innerBoundaryIs>`. A ring that is not closed SHALL be closed by repeating its first point, and that SHALL NOT be an error.
- R-38 (ubiquitous): A candidate ring SHALL pass every rule of Table D before it is stored: at least 3 distinct vertices, at most 5,000 vertices, a non zero absolute area, no self intersection between any two non adjacent segments, every longitude within -180 to 180 and every latitude within -90 to 90, an area of at most 2,000 ha, and an overlap of at most 1 percent of the smaller of the two rings with every registered field and with every crop polygon of block 5 rendered at the current view. The area SHALL be the same spherical area block 6 computes for its `ring_area` metric, and the overlap SHALL be the intersection area divided by the smaller of the two rings' areas, times 100, rounded to one decimal.
- R-39 (ubiquitous): Every rejection SHALL print exactly one error string from Table D through `t()`, in the card and next to the control that caused it, SHALL keep the ring in the editor so it can be corrected, SHALL NOT write any local storage key, and SHALL NOT discard the other fields. The strings are part of this contract and SHALL NOT be reworded in a component.
- R-40 (ubiquitous): A valid ring SHALL be stored as one record with an id of the form `local:` followed by a lower case UUID version 4 (for example `local:8f14e45f-ceea-467f-a4c6-1e6b0c2b0c11`), a name of 1 to 60 characters that is unique among the registered fields, the ring, the computed area in hectares, the source (`drawn`, `geojson` or `kml`), the imported file name when there is one, `region` and the two timestamps of Table C. `region` SHALL be `br` or `us` when the ring's centroid falls inside the region extent of Table C, and `null` otherwise; a field with a null region SHALL print `No region source for this boundary` where a region source is needed, and SHALL NOT be rejected.
- R-41 (ubiquitous): A registered field SHALL be a selectable field of the app: a press on its map polygon or on its chip SHALL run the registered command `select_field` with its `local:` id through `runCommand`, its polygon SHALL be drawn from its own MapLibre source, its chip SHALL render in the block 3 `Fields` chip row after the fixture fields, and it SHALL be the query area of the block 5 and block 6 paths, which receive its ring, centroid and area exactly as they receive a polygon's. While the field's `region` differs from the active `regionId`, or is `null`, the press SHALL select nothing, SHALL print `Switch the region to {region} to open this field.` for a known region and the text of R-40 for a null one, and SHALL NOT change the active region, because block 5 R-03 makes `?region=` and nothing else the region selector.
- R-42 (ubiquitous): Deleting a registered field SHALL require one confirmation carrying the field name, SHALL remove its record and every producer record keyed by its id in the same action, SHALL clear the selection when that field is selected, and SHALL leave every fixture field untouched. The fixture fields SHALL NOT be deletable through any control of this block.

### Declared management history (F-22)

- R-43 (ubiquitous): The declared management history SHALL hold one row per registered or fixture field and per season, with the keys of Table C: `season` in the block 4 `Y/YY` form, `first_crop`, `second_crop` (nullable), `sowing_date` (nullable), `tillage` (nullable, one of `no_till`, `minimum_till`, `conventional`), `cover_crop` (nullable, 60 characters at most), `basis` (always `entered`) and `edited_at`. A row SHALL be entered by the producer and SHALL NOT be seeded, copied from the fixture or pre filled with a plausible value.
- R-44 (ubiquitous): Every history row SHALL carry the basis `entered`, the field id and the instant of its last edit, and that basis SHALL print beside the row. No history row SHALL be derived from block 6's MapBiomas rotation, from the NDVI series, from the scenario list, from the fixture crop history or from a previous row of the same field.
- R-45 (state-driven): While a field has no history row, the card SHALL print its empty state naming that nothing is declared, the comparator's `residue` and `nitrogen` criteria SHALL have no score, and no crop, season, tillage or cover value SHALL print for that field.
- R-46 (ubiquitous): The declared history and block 6's MapBiomas rotation SHALL print as two separate lists under two headings, one with the `entered` basis and one with the `modelled` basis, and SHALL NOT be merged, sorted into one sequence or reconciled. Where the two disagree for the same season, both SHALL print and the card SHALL print that they disagree; the app SHALL NOT choose one and SHALL NOT relabel either.
- R-47 (ubiquitous): A history row SHALL be editable and deletable one row at a time. `first_crop` SHALL be required; a `sowing_date` SHALL fall inside its own season (1 July of the first year to 30 June of the second) or the row SHALL be rejected with the exact text of Table D; a `second_crop` SHALL NOT equal `first_crop`; and a duplicate season for the same field SHALL be rejected, not merged.

### Economic constraints (F-23)

- R-48 (ubiquitous): F-23 SHALL enter with exactly two fields and no third. `cost_per_ha` is the declared cost of one scenario in the currency and area unit of the field's own region, Brazilian reais per hectare in Brazil and United States dollars per acre in the United States; `product_price` is the declared price of one crop in Brazilian reais per 60 kilogram sack in Brazil and United States dollars per bushel in the United States, declared per crop. Both SHALL be entered per scenario and per crop respectively on the `Economics` card, SHALL be stored under the keys of Table C, and SHALL carry the `entered` basis and the edit date.
- R-49 (ubiquitous): Each kept field SHALL have exactly the consumer named here, and the criterion named here SHALL fail when that consumer is gone. `cost_per_ha` feeds the `income` derivation of R-19 and the printed `break_even` of its scenario: the criterion asserts that changing the declared cost changes the printed `income` score and the printed total. `product_price` feeds the same `break_even`, which is `cost_per_ha / price` in sacks per hectare in Brazil and in bushels per acre in the United States: the criterion asserts that changing the declared price changes the printed `break_even` and the `income` score. A field with no consumer in this spec SHALL NOT be added, and a control that only stores a value SHALL NOT be added.
- R-50 (unwanted behaviour): The `lease term` field of F-23 SHALL NOT be built. This spec holds no declared investment, no payback horizon and no multi year sequence to compare a term against, so any comparison would invent the horizon it pretends to measure. The field is listed in Out of scope, carried as CO-B7-04, and SHALL NOT appear in the card, in the stored schema or in the payload.
- R-51 (state-driven): While a scenario has no declared `cost_per_ha` or its last crop has no declared `product_price`, its `break_even` SHALL be `null`, the `income` score SHALL be `null`, the card SHALL print the abstention text naming the missing declaration, and no zero, default price, national average or fixture value SHALL stand in.

### Local persistence

- R-52 (ubiquitous): All producer data of this block SHALL be stored in the browser's `localStorage` under exactly two keys, `fs.fields.v1` for the registered fields and `fs.producer.v1` for the weights, scores, declarations, history and economics, each with `schema_version` 1 at the top and both following the prefix of the one key the app already writes, `fs.locale` in `src/strings/index.ts`. No other key SHALL be written, no server SHALL be contacted, and no real database SHALL be read or written.
- R-53 (ubiquitous): A write SHALL happen only on an explicit action (saving a field, editing a history row, changing a weight, declaring a cost or a price, deleting), SHALL replace the whole key in one `setItem` call carrying the complete current content, and SHALL NOT happen on load, on mount, on a resize or on a surface switch. A field deletion SHALL write the producer key first and the fields key second, so that a failure of the second write leaves a field with no producer rows and never producer rows with no field. A stored record SHALL be validated on read, and a record that does not match Table C SHALL be treated as unreadable under R-54.
- R-54 (unwanted behaviour): If a stored key carries a `schema_version` other than 1, or a record that fails the shape of Table C, then the app SHALL NOT read it, SHALL NOT migrate it, SHALL print `The stored record was written by a different version and is not read`, one gap row and no value from it, and SHALL keep the raw string in place until the producer exports or resets it.
- R-55 (unwanted behaviour): If a write raises a quota error, the app SHALL leave the previous value of that key intact, SHALL print `The browser refused to store the field. Free space and try again.`, SHALL keep the entered values on screen so nothing is lost, and SHALL NOT drop a record silently or write a partial record.
- R-56 (ubiquitous): The `Producer data` card SHALL export both keys as one JSON download named `field-shift-producer-{date}.json`, with `schema_version` and the export date, and SHALL reset both keys after one confirmation that names what is deleted. Any move of these records to a server database SHALL need the user's explicit approval, a recorded decision and its own spec; nothing in this block authorises it, and no route of this block writes a record anywhere but local storage.

### Honesty, gaps and the fixture

- R-57 (ubiquitous): Every number this block prints SHALL be one of four kinds, and each kind SHALL print its own provenance: a block 6 metric, with the row and the basis of that metric; a ZARC limit, with its portaria and its date; a declared value, with the `entered` basis and the edit date; a simulated value, with the `simulated` mark, the source of its input series and the transformation. A value computed from declared values, such as `break_even` or an `income` score, SHALL print its declared inputs and the arithmetic beside it and SHALL carry the `modelled` basis, not `entered`. A number that is none of the four kinds fails this requirement.
- R-58 (ubiquitous): Each surface SHALL carry one gap register with one row per gap, each row naming the surface, the cause, the source or key that failed, the status or error text, the date and the remediation (the carry-over id where one exists). A gap row SHALL appear only when its condition is measured, and with no gap the register SHALL print its empty text.
- R-59 (ubiquitous): No control of this block SHALL set, clear or override the basis label, value or date of any row of block 6, and no label of this block SHALL be derived by matching a pattern in a metric id, a layer name, a scenario name or a source string. The weights, the scores and the simulator reorder and recompute; they never relabel.
- R-60 (ubiquitous): The three fixture fields SHALL stay the demonstration mode and SHALL be read only in this block. No requirement of this block SHALL write, rename or delete a fixture field, and `backend/fixtures/field-scenarios.json` SHALL NOT change.

### Table A: the criteria

Every score of this block is one row of this table. The `basis` column is the only source of a row's basis label; a criterion id, a scenario name or a source string changes nothing.

| Criterion id | Label key | Direction | Score source | Unit | Basis | Derivation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `water` | `decision.criterion.water` | higher is better | block 6 metric `rain_percentile` | percent | `modelled` | R-19, field level, shared by every scenario |
| `income` | `decision.criterion.income` | higher is better | derived from declared `cost_per_ha` and `product_price` | index | `modelled` | R-19, `100 * min(break_even) / break_even` |
| `residue` | `decision.criterion.residue` | higher is better | producer declaration only | index | `entered` | none, and the reason is R-19 |

A score row carries exactly these keys: `criterion`, `value` (integer 0 to 100 or null), `basis`, `source`, `as_of`, `edited_at`. ### Table B: the formula, in the order the card prints it

| Step | Rule | Printed |
|---|---|---|
| eligible set | every criterion whose weight is above zero | the four weights and their sum |
| scored set of a scenario | its eligible criteria that have a score | the four cells of the scenario row, each with its basis and date |
| coverage | `round(100 * sum of the weights of the scored set / sum of the weights of the eligible set)` | `coverage = 100 * 25 / 100 = 25 percent (threshold 80)` |
| rankable | coverage at or above 80 | `ranked` or `not ranked` |
| total | `sum(w * n) / sum(w)` over the scored set, one decimal, round half up | `total = (100 * 20) / 100 = 20.0` |
| order | descending total, ties share the lowest rank, competition ranks, array order inside a tie | the rank number or `tie` |
| no eligible criterion | every weight is zero | `Every weight is zero` |
| fewer than two rankable | the notice of R-16 | no rank, no total |

Worked example with numbers this repository holds. Field `mt-santa-carmem-01`, chosen date 2026-11-12, default weights 25 for each criterion.

- `water` = 20. This is the block 6 metric `rain_percentile`: the 2026 window 2026-10-01 to 2026-11-11 totals 171.93 mm, seven of the 35 earlier years are lower (`1996` 162.11, `1997` 137.24, `2014` 135.78, `2015` 164.27, `2016` 121.64, `2020` 88.87, `2025` 109.46), so `rank` is 7 and `round(100 * 7 / 35)` is 20. Recomputed on this card on 2026-11-14 (O-05).
- `income`, `residue` and `nitrogen` have no score: no cost, no price and no declaration exist for this field.
- Eligible weight sum 100. Scored weight sum for each of the three scenarios is 25, because only `water` is scored. Coverage is `round(100 * 25 / 100)` = 25, below 80. No scenario is rankable, so the card prints no rank and no total, keeps the three cards in fixture order, and names `income, residue, nitrogen` as the missing criteria. That is the honest state of the fixture and the state the criterion for R-15 measures.

Second worked example, still with numbers this repository holds. Weights `water` 100 and the other three 0. The eligible weight sum is 100 and the scored weight sum is 100, so coverage is 100 and every scenario is rankable. The total is `(100 * 20) / 100 = 20.0` for all three scenarios, so all three share rank 1 with the mark `tie`, printed in fixture order: `Current sequence`, `Soybean → Corn cover`, `Soybean → Cotton`.

Third worked example, which shows the reordering and uses declared values, marked `entered` and dated 2026-11-14, not measurements: `income` 74 for `Current sequence`, 86 for `Soybean → Corn cover`, 62 for `Soybean → Cotton`; `nitrogen` 30, 40 and 90. With `income` 100 and `nitrogen` 0 the totals are 74.0, 86.0 and 62.0, so the order is corn cover, current, cotton. With `income` 0 and `nitrogen` 100 the totals are 30.0, 40.0 and 90.0, so the order is cotton, corn cover, current. The order changes because the two criteria disagree, and it changes the same way on every run.
