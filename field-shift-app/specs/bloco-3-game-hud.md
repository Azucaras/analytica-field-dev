## Problem

Block 2 made the map full screen and anchored every box around its edges, but it left the screen busy in three ways. The agent command form is always mounted in the bottom centre, so a text form owns the place where the eye rests. The rotation outlook sits on the left edge and shows five paragraphs of text per scenario before any visual, so the comparison the panel exists for is buried in prose. The supporting data table is permanently open in the bottom right even though it is the least actionable box on screen. On top of that the coverage chip in the top right repeats a number that the rotation heading already prints, and the map navigation control sits in the top right where it covers the Season select.

Block 3 turns the HUD into a game HUD: primary actions become toggles, secondary information goes behind those toggles, the rotation outlook becomes a visual front card with a text back card, and every control that is not the map moves to the periphery.

## Users and scope

- Users: the producer who operates the map and the judge who evaluates the screen.
- In scope: command toggle, collapsible field list with the coss primitive vendored, rotation outlook as a bottom front/back card with two new visuals, supporting data toggle, removal of the top right coverage chip and of every other repeated coverage readout, map navigation control moved to centre right, the resulting DOM, accessibility and layout contracts, and the carry-over register.
- Out of scope: every item in Carry-over, any change to the fixture data or the backend contract, any new command, any change to the layer presets, any typographic change, any map label, any new chart beyond the two named in R-14 and R-15.

## Game UX rationale

Source: Game UX.md, the Wild Rift translation (thumb zone hierarchy, clear centre, progressive disclosure, peripheral anchoring).

| Change | Wild Rift principle | One line |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Command becomes a toggle | Progressive disclosure, primary action on demand | The form is the ability you cast, not furniture; it appears when invoked and leaves when the cast is done. |
| 2. Fields become collapsible | Clear centre, peripheral list | The field list is a roster in the corner; it expands on demand and collapses to the one active field, like a scoreboard that shows only your champion. |
| 3. Rotation outlook moves to the bottom, sits in one row and flips | Peripheral anchoring plus progressive disclosure | The three comparisons sit side by side at the bottom edge, bars answer "what do the numbers say" at a glance, and the reasoning sits one press behind them, the way an enemy item build sits behind the scoreboard. The card itself is the press and lights on hover, so no furniture is spent on a button. |
| 4. Supporting data becomes a toggle | Secondary information behind a toggle | The table is a curiosity, so it starts closed and never competes with the outlook for attention. |
| 5. Coverage chip removed | Quiet everywhere except one place | The same number printed twice is noise; the rotation heading keeps the single authoritative copy. |
| 6. Navigation control to centre right | Thumb zone hierarchy, keep the top edge for context | Zoom and compass sit on the right edge at hand height, clear of the top strip where season context lives. |

## Requirements

Types follow block 2: ubiquitous, state-driven, event-driven, unwanted behaviour.

### Command toggle

- R-01 (ubiquitous): The agent command form SHALL NOT be mounted at first paint; it SHALL mount only after the user activates the `Command` button, and activating the same button again SHALL unmount it.
- R-02 (ubiquitous): The `Command` button SHALL be a native `button` element with the accessible name `Command`, anchored in the top right strip immediately left of the `Supporting data` button, which itself sits immediately left of the Season control, with a minimum hit area of 44 by 44 px. It SHALL render as the strip's primary action, with the fill the app already uses for a primary button: `background-color` `#f2f6ea`, a 1 px `#dfe8d1` border, a 6 px radius and the label colour `#465d32`, which are the declarations `.agent-command button` already carries in `src/index.css`. It is the only filled control in the strip, and that fill is what AC-14 measures `Supporting data` against; without a stated fill the comparison would have no ground.
- R-03 (state-driven): While the form is open the `Command` button SHALL expose `aria-expanded="true"` and `aria-controls` pointing at the form id; while closed it SHALL expose `aria-expanded="false"` and no dangling id reference. The form SHALL keep the accessible name `Agent command`.
- R-04 (event-driven): When the form opens, focus SHALL move to the `Command name` text box; when it closes, focus SHALL return to the `Command` button. Closing SHALL NOT discard the typed command or the typed JSON arguments.

### Fields panel

- R-05 (ubiquitous): The field list SHALL be wrapped in the coss `collapsible` primitive, vendored at `src/components/ui/collapsible.tsx` from the registry item `@coss/collapsible` (`https://coss.com/ui/r/collapsible.json`, already reachable through the `@coss` registry entry in `components.json`). Its only dependency is `@base-ui/react`, already installed at `^1.9.0` in `package.json`, so no new package is needed. The supported route is the exact file in Annex A, vendored by hand. The CLI is best effort: `npx shadcn@4.23.0 add @coss/collapsible` matches the `shadcn` version installed in `node_modules` (4.23.0), but the registry rewrite is not verified here, so if the command fails, Annex A is the route. The module SHALL export `Collapsible`, `CollapsibleTrigger` and `CollapsiblePanel` (also exported as `CollapsibleContent`) and SHALL import `cn` from `@/lib/utils`. No other collapsible or accordion module SHALL be introduced.
- R-06 (ubiquitous): The fields trigger SHALL be a native `button` with the accessible name `Fields`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` pointing at the chip row id, and a minimum hit area of 44 by 44 px.
- R-07 (state-driven): The field panel SHALL start collapsed at first paint. While expanded, one chip SHALL render per field. While collapsed and a field is selected, exactly one chip SHALL render in the fields header beside the `Fields` trigger and outside the controlled chip row: the selected field, carrying its name only, so the active field is never lost; while the list is still loading and nothing is selected, the row SHALL render no chip rather than a placeholder that looks selected. The chip SHALL NOT print the coverage percentage (R-26).
- R-08 (state-driven): Collapsing or expanding the field panel SHALL NOT change the selected field, SHALL NOT reset the map camera, and SHALL NOT issue a new scenario request.
- R-09 (unwanted behaviour): If the field request has failed, the collapsed panel SHALL keep the error text and the Retry control reachable through the expanded panel, and SHALL NOT render a chip for a field that is not in the loaded list.

### Rotation outlook card

- R-10 (ubiquitous): The rotation outlook panel SHALL anchor to the bottom of the screen per the layout contract, SHALL keep the heading `Rotation outlook` with its eyebrow `PLAN WITH EVIDENCE`. The `field-coverage-pill` inside that heading row is owned by R-26 and is not restated here.
- R-11 (state-driven): Each rotation scenario SHALL render as one card with two faces, and exactly one face SHALL be exposed at a time: the front at first paint, the back after the flip control is activated. The three cards SHALL sit in one horizontal row in fixture array order, per R-42.
- R-12 (ubiquitous): The front face SHALL render the SMAP visual of R-14 and the crop history visual of R-15. The scenario name, that is the card's `h3` heading, and the current badge when the scenario is current SHALL stay visible on both faces: they render once in the card chrome above the face content and are not duplicated inside either face, and the flip control of R-18 overlays the body without swallowing either of them in the accessibility tree. The front SHALL NOT render the why, evidence, benefit, gap or risk text.
- R-13 (ubiquitous): The back face SHALL render the five facts in the block 2 order (why, evidence, benefit, gap, risk) with the same labels and the same source strings. The scenario name and the current badge stay visible from the card chrome as R-12 states. The back SHALL NOT render the two visuals.
- R-14 (ubiquitous): The SMAP visual SHALL be one horizontal bar sized from the scenario evidence row whose layer is `SMAP soil moisture`, using the value string exactly as the fixture returns it, in the unit the fixture carries (`m³/m³`). The bar track SHALL be scaled from 0 to `SMAP_SCALE_MAX`, a single named constant with the default 0.5 m³/m³. The filled state SHALL occur only for a row whose basis is `observed`, `retrieved` or `entered` and whose `value` parses to a finite number; every other row draws the empty state of R-17. In the filled state the numeric value, the basis badge and the as of date SHALL render as text next to the bar, so the bar is never the only carrier of the value.
- R-15 (ubiquitous): The crop history visual SHALL render one bar per entry of the scenario `crops` array, in array order, each bar labelled with the crop name, with equal length for every crop because the fixture carries no per crop quantity. The basis badge and the as of date SHALL come from the `Crop history` row when the scenario has one; when it has none, the bars still render and no basis or date is printed, because nothing may be invented. The visual SHALL NOT encode a length, area, yield or season count that the fixture does not supply, and no bar SHALL carry a numeric label, so the as of date is the only numeric text this group prints.
- R-16 (state-driven): When the scenario has no `SMAP soil moisture` row, the SMAP visual SHALL render the track empty with the text `Not reported for this option`, which is the new catalog key `visual.not_reported` because no existing key carries that wording, and SHALL NOT inherit a value, a basis or an as of date from another scenario: the fill width SHALL be exactly 0 px, and the SMAP group SHALL print no basis badge and no date, because the row that would carry them does not exist. The fixture's `soy-cotton` scenario is this state at every field.
- R-17 (state-driven): The drawn or empty state of the SMAP bar SHALL be a pure function of the row's own `basis` and of whether its `value` parses to a finite number; no control and no other row SHALL set it. Whenever that function does not yield a measured row the fill width SHALL be exactly 0 px, the track SHALL stay visible and empty, and a literal label SHALL print, with the row's own basis badge where the row exists, for these states: basis `modelled` prints `SMAP soil moisture (modelled)`, which is the new catalog key `evidence.layer_modelled` with the template `{layer} (modelled)`, and in that state the numeric string SHALL NOT print anywhere in the SMAP group, because a value that was modelled is not a measurement; value null prints `No observation`; value `Not entered` prints `Not entered`; no row prints `Not reported for this option` under R-16. The numeric ban is scoped to the SMAP group alone: the crop history group on the same face still prints its as of date under R-15, so the two requirements never conflict. A bar that draws any length for a modelled, missing or absent value fails this requirement, and so does a filled bar for a measured row that withholds its number.
- R-18 (ubiquitous): The flip control SHALL be a native `button` that covers the whole card body: a transparent, borderless element positioned over the body, at least 44 by 44 px, a sibling of the face content rather than a wrapper around it, so the heading, the current badge, the five facts and the two visual groups stay exposed in the accessibility tree instead of being flattened into a button subtree. It SHALL carry no visible text of its own: the visible `Details` and `Charts` labels of the earlier revision are retired and the two catalog keys `visual.flip_details_label` and `visual.flip_charts_label` SHALL be deleted in the same change. The control SHALL expose `aria-expanded="false"` while the front shows and `aria-expanded="true"` while the back shows, and `aria-controls` pointing at the card body id. Its accessible name SHALL be `Charts for {scenario name}` while the front shows and `Details for {scenario name}` while the back shows, so the name states the face currently on screen rather than the destination. `{scenario name}` SHALL be the card's rendered heading, which is the translated `scenario.name` of the fixture: `Current sequence`, `Soybean → Corn cover` or `Soybean → Cotton`. It is not the `Crop history` row value (`Soybean → Corn`) and not the kicker (`Option Soybean / Cotton`). `aria-pressed` SHALL NOT be used, because a pressed state needs a stable label and this control's name changes with the face. The affordance that the card can be turned SHALL be the card's own background: a `:hover` rule and a `:focus-within` rule SHALL give the card element a computed `background-color` that differs from that same card's resting value, and no other control or badge SHALL be added to signal it. On a touch screen there is no hover, so the cue never appears there; the card still flips on tap, and that gap is stated here rather than papered over with a control the user rejected.
- R-19 (event-driven): Activating the flip control SHALL NOT move focus off the control, SHALL NOT scroll the document, SHALL NOT change the card row's `scrollLeft`, and SHALL NOT change the bottom edge of the rotation panel.
- R-20 (state-driven): The face swap SHALL be instant for every user: no 3D transform, no crossfade, no height animation, computed transition duration 0 s on the face container, and the outgoing face SHALL be unmounted in the same commit, so the card DOM holds exactly one face at any moment. A CSS hidden face is not sufficient, because a verifier must be able to prove the absence of the other face from a DOM query and not only from the accessibility tree. This satisfies `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` by construction, so no motion branch is required.

### Supporting data toggle

- R-21 (ubiquitous): The evidence panel SHALL NOT be mounted at first paint; the `Supporting data` button, a native `button` element with the accessible name `Supporting data`, SHALL mount it and unmount it. While closed the button SHALL expose `aria-expanded="false"` and no `aria-controls` reference that fails to resolve; while open it SHALL expose `aria-expanded="true"` and `aria-controls` pointing at the mounted panel, and the mounted panel SHALL keep the accessible name `Evidence` and the same five column table.
- R-22 (ubiquitous): The `Supporting data` button SHALL render as a secondary control, defined absolutely rather than by comparison: a fully transparent computed `background-color` (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)`), a computed `border-top-width` of 0 px, no box shadow, and the strip's ordinary label colour `#25372a`, which is the colour `.top-chip` already sets. Against the filled `#f2f6ea`, `#dfe8d1` and `#465d32` primary style that R-02 pins on `Command`, that is the whole of the difference, and it is what AC-14 measures. It SHALL sit in the top right strip immediately right of `Command` and immediately left of the Season control, with a minimum hit area of 44 by 44 px.
- R-23 (ubiquitous): The command form and the evidence panel SHALL open in one right edge column, in the order `Command` above `Supporting data`, separated by an 8 px gap, and the second panel SHALL sit below the first when both are open. The status notice keeps a single mount point at every width, the top level overlay it has today, and there is never a second instance, so the notice is not a child of the column and the relation is geometric. At 1440x900 and 780x900 the notice SHALL render at right 22, top 60, width min(400, 45vw), and the right edge column SHALL start 8 px below the notice's bottom edge whenever the notice is mounted, so no panel can cover it. At 780x900 the notice SHALL be capped at 112 px tall with internal scroll, so its bottom edge stays above the clear centre rectangle's top edge at y 180 (R-31). When the column's content exceeds its maximum height it SHALL scroll internally instead of growing. At 1440x900 and 780x900 the column SHALL also keep its bottom edge at or above y 393, which is 8 px above the navigation control's top edge at y 401 (`50vh - 49px`), scrolling internally rather than growing past that edge, so the column and the navigation control never share area. At 360x640 the column is bounded by R-40 instead.
- R-24 (ubiquitous): Neither the command form nor the evidence panel SHALL cover the map centre point (50 percent of width, 50 percent of height) at 1440x900 or 780x900 while open. The clear centre rectangle of R-31 is a first paint rule, so a panel may enter it while it is open and must leave with the map centre untouched.

### Coverage chip

- R-25 (ubiquitous): The top right `COVERAGE` chip SHALL be removed from the DOM, its `.coverage-chip` rule SHALL be removed from `src/index.css`, and the string key `map.coverage_label` SHALL be deleted if no other consumer remains. The CSS deletion is a code review item: no criterion can measure the absence of a CSS rule, and AC-19 proves only that the element and the label string are gone from the DOM.
- R-26 (ubiquitous): Coverage SHALL be printed in exactly one element of the UI: the `field-coverage-pill` in the rotation heading, which the user named as the place where the number already appears. Consequences that are binding: the per field percentage inside each field chip SHALL be removed (the chip shows the field name only), and the abstention message SHALL stop printing the percentage by switching from `notice.not_enough_valid_area` (`Not enough valid area ({coverage}% of 80% required)`) to the existing key `notice.ranking_withheld_period`, composed with `notice.coverage_below_80_percent` as its `threshold` parameter (`Ranking withheld: coverage below 80%.`). The `80%` in that message is the backend coverage threshold, not the selected field's coverage readout, so its percent sign is not a second copy of the one authoritative number and does not violate this rule. The only live renderer of this message is `src/components/AbstentionNotice.tsx`, in its `threshold === 'coverage_below_80_percent'` branch, which the notice overlay in `src/App.tsx` mounts; that is the file this change touches. `src/components/ScenarioCards.tsx` composes the same catalog key in its own abstention branch, but that branch is unreachable because `src/App.tsx` passes `state` and `threshold` as null to it whenever the response is abstained, so `ScenarioCards.tsx` SHALL NOT be edited for this requirement and an implementer who edits it will see no rendered change. The key `notice.not_enough_valid_area` SHALL then be deleted if no other consumer remains, and every test that asserts the old text or interpolates the deleted key SHALL be updated in the same change (see Consequence 1). This one place rule is asserted by AC-20 and no exception is permitted without an amendment to this requirement. Deleting the `notice.not_enough_valid_area` key from the catalog is a code review item, because no criterion can observe a key that nothing references; AC-20 measures the rendered literal and the removed percentage.

### Navigation control

- R-27 (ubiquitous): The MapLibre `NavigationControl` SHALL render at the vertical centre of the right edge, inside the map container, with the control's right edge 16 px from the viewport right edge and its vertical centre within 2 px of 50 percent of the viewport height. The zoom in, zoom out and compass buttons SHALL stay in that control.
- R-28 (unwanted behaviour): The navigation control SHALL NOT intersect the Season control, the top right chip strip, the field chip strip, the left stack (the fields panel and the status notice), the command form, the evidence panel, the rotation panel or the layer rail, at 1440x900, 780x900 and 360x640, and SHALL also not intersect the attribution control at 1440x900 and 780x900. At 360x640 the attribution control sits at mid screen (CO-02), so the navigation control at the vertical centre lands on it; that overlap is carried in CO-02 and is not claimed as fixed here. At 360x640 the zero intersection with the other boxes is held by the 64 px navigation gutter that R-39 and R-40 give the left stack, the right edge column and the layer rail, not by a corner anchor. Intersection means a positive area overlap.
- R-29 (ubiquitous): The Season select SHALL stay in the top right strip, SHALL remain reachable at all three widths, and SHALL open its listbox when clicked. At 360x640 reachable means the strip is scrolled to its end, after which the select is hit testable at its own centre; at 1440x900 and 780x900 no scrolling is required.

### Layout and shell

### DOM, strings and data

- R-36 (ubiquitous): The block 2 role and name pairs SHALL survive as listed in the DOM contract, and that list now holds six pairs, four unconditional and two conditional on their toggle being open. One of block 2's seven is withdrawn by this revision, the form pair AM-13 names, which is exactly the contract change this requirement reserves for an amendment; any other change to those pairs is a contract change and needs its own amendment here.
- R-37 (ubiquitous): Every new visible string and every new accessible name SHALL come from the string catalog (`src/strings/en.ts`), added in the same change. No literal text SHALL be added to a component. - R-38 (ubiquitous): The two visuals SHALL read only the existing scenario response. No new endpoint, field, unit or derived number SHALL be invented; where the fixture has no value the states of R-16 and R-17 apply.
- R-39 (ubiquitous): At 360x640 the fields panel SHALL be one left flow column on its own: a single normal flow column at `top: 58px; left: 8px; right: 64px`, with no second box in that column, vertical overflow inside the column when the content exceeds the viewport. The status notice keeps its single top level overlay mount (R-23) and renders in the same band at `top: 58px; left: 8px; right: 64px`; while it is shown the flow column SHALL start 8 px below its bottom edge, so the visual order is notice then fields panel, and the two boxes SHALL NOT share area in any combination of expanded or collapsed and notice shown or hidden. The expanded fields panel SHALL be exactly two rows, the 44 px header and one 44 px chip row with an 11 px gap, and the chip row SHALL scroll horizontally and SHALL NOT wrap; the collapsed panel SHALL be the header only, 44 px. The notice SHALL keep a maximum height of 8vh. The 64 px right inset is the navigation gutter: at 360 no box in that band, the notice included, SHALL enter the navigation control's rectangle.
- R-40 (ubiquitous): At 360x640 the command form and the evidence panel SHALL share one right edge column that sits entirely above the rotation panel with an 8 px gap, at `left: 8px; right: 64px`, with a maximum height of 104 px and internal scroll. Only one of the two SHALL be open at a time at that width: opening one SHALL close the other. At 1440x900 and 780x900 both MAY be open and they stack per R-23. The transition across the 360x640 breakpoint is defined rather than left to the opening rule: when the viewport becomes 360 while both panels are open, the panel that was opened last SHALL stay open, the other SHALL close, and focus SHALL stay where it was; leaving 360 SHALL not reopen the closed panel, so what the user sees after the transition is what remains. This is the only resize transition that changes a toggle state (R-35).
- R-41 (ubiquitous): Each of the four toggle controls, the `Command` button, the `Supporting data` button, the `Fields` trigger and each card's flip control, SHALL be a native `button` element reachable with Tab, and SHALL toggle on Enter and on Space exactly as a press does: the controlled region SHALL mount or unmount, or the face SHALL swap, and the control's own `aria-expanded` SHALL flip, with focus staying on that control. No other key SHALL toggle it. An element that is not a button and carries `role="button"` with a click handler does not satisfy this requirement, because it would not answer the keyboard, and a role query alone cannot tell the two apart, so the criterion also asserts the element's tag name. The `Command` button is the single exception to the focus clause: R-04 governs it, so the key that opens the form moves focus into the `Command name` box and the key that closes it returns focus to the button, which means a verifier SHALL re-focus the button before the second press. Every other clause of this requirement applies to `Command` unchanged: the native tag, the Tab reachability, the two keys and the refusal of any other key.
- R-42 (ubiquitous): The three rotation cards SHALL lay out in one horizontal row of equal columns in fixture array order. The list SHALL use `grid-auto-flow: column` with `grid-auto-columns: minmax(240px, 1fr)` and the existing 10 px gap, and SHALL NOT stack the cards vertically or wrap them onto a second row. When the columns do not fit the panel, the row SHALL scroll horizontally instead of shrinking a card under 240 px or stacking it. The cause this removes is measured, not guessed: `src/index.css` line 327 declares `.scenario-list { grid-template-columns: 1fr; }` in the main overlay scope with no media query, so it overrides the three column rule at line 234 at every width, and at 1440x900 the computed track is a single 950 px column inside a list that measures about 952 px inside the panel's 16 px padding, where three columns fit. The card face rules of R-11 to R-13 are unchanged by this layout.

## Expected Interface

Public names and the layout contract only.

- `@/components/ui/collapsible`: `Collapsible`, `CollapsibleTrigger`, `CollapsiblePanel` (alias `CollapsibleContent`), from the coss registry item `@coss/collapsible`.
- `FieldsPanel`: wraps `Collapsible` and owns the `Fields` trigger, the chip row and the collapsed selected chip.
- `RotationFlipCard`: one scenario card with `front` and `back` faces, the overlay flip control of R-18 covering the card body, and its `aria-controls` wiring; it renders no visible flip label.
- Rotation card row: the `ul` that holds the cards, laid out per R-42. No new component is needed.
- `SmapBar`: one bar, props `value` (string or null), `basis`, `asOf`, `scaleMax` (number). The drawn or empty state is derived inside the component from `basis` and `value` (R-14, R-17); no prop carries an assumption.
- `CropHistoryBars`: one bar per crop, props `crops: string[]`, `basis`, `asOf`.
- `SupportingDataToggle`: the `Supporting data` button plus the mounted `EvidencePanel`.
- `CommandToggle`: the `Command` button plus the mounted `AgentBar` form.
- `ZOrder`: existing export, keys kept, plus `controlStrip: 60`; `agentBar` becomes 70. The `scenarioCards` key keeps its name and now orders the rotation panel.
- Kept unchanged in public name: `MapCanvas`, `LayerRail`, `ScenarioCards`, `EvidencePanel`, `SeasonSelect`, `AbstentionNotice`, `AgentBar`. The form of block 1 is withdrawn by AM-11 and has no public name left to keep.
- `MapCanvas`: keeps `NavigationControl`, mounted into a centre right container instead of `top-right`.

Fixture facts that shape the AC:

1. The `soy-cotton` option carries only `Rotation suitability` and `Market outlet` rows. It has no SMAP row, so its SMAP visual shows the not reported state, and no `Crop history` row, so its crop bars render from `crops` (two crops) without a basis badge or a date. That is the honest render of the fixture, not a defect.
2. The crop history row value is a text sequence, not a series. The front therefore shows sequence bars with uniform length. A quantity bar (area, yield, seasons per crop) requires new fixture fields and is OD-06.
3. The fixture carries no SMAP maximum, no per field threshold and no saturation value. The bar scale is therefore a named constant, not a measured value: 0.5 m³/m³ as `SMAP_SCALE_MAX`. That constant is an assumption of this spec, not a fixture value and not part of C-03: C-03 says only how an empty or assumed bar is drawn. 0.5 is chosen so the three observed values occupy 38, 56 and 62 percent of the track, which keeps them distinguishable; no per field threshold is displayed, and the 80 percent coverage threshold of the backend is not a moisture threshold, so it SHALL NOT be drawn on this bar. The constant is listed as OD-08.
4. Every SMAP row in the fixture is a populated `retrieved` string, and there are six of them. No SMAP row carries a null value, the literal `Not entered` or the basis `modelled`, although `value` is typed `string | null` and `basis` carries four values, in `src/components/ScenarioCards.tsx` and `src/components/EvidencePanel.tsx`. The null, `Not entered` and `modelled` states are therefore proven by a component test that renders `SmapBar` with an injected row (AC-13), while the measured `retrieved` state and the missing row state of `soy-cotton` are measured in the browser on this fixture. No new fixture data is needed for any of the four states.
