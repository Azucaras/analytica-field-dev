# AC-21 (R-06, R-26) rail row, 1440x900, computer use

Method: the real app in Chromium at 1440x900, dev server 127.0.0.1:5199, backend
127.0.0.1:8001, chosen date at its default (2026-11-14, the axis end). Rectangles are
`getBoundingClientRect()` readouts in CSS px. The card forbids PNGs, so the `innerText`
dump and the rectangle table below stand in for the screenshot the spec names.

## Collapsed rows, `innerText` (first two)

```
SMAP soil moisture | retrieved | 9 km | 2026-11-11 | 3 days old | earlier observation | No observation
SMAP L4 surface soil moisture | modelled | 9 km | 2026-11-10 | 4 days old | earlier observation | No observation
```

Field by field: the layer name, its basis, its resolution, its effective date, its age, its
status, and the R-06 marker. The marker is present on both because their effective dates
differ from the chosen date 2026-11-14. The layered `innerText` of the whole rail lists the
three presets first, then the thirteen rows in stack order.

## Expanded row of `smap-l3`, boxes in px

```
Layer options, SMAP soil moisture (row trigger)    44.00 x 44.00
Show SMAP soil moisture (on/off switch)            44.00 x 44.00
Opacity, SMAP soil moisture (range)               251.00 x 44.00
Move SMAP soil moisture up                         44.00 x 44.00
Move SMAP soil moisture down                       44.00 x 44.00
Pin date, SMAP soil moisture                      251.00 x 44.00
Compare, SMAP soil moisture                       251.00 x 44.00
SMAP soil moisture availability (the track)       251.00 x 10.00
```

Every control in the expanded row measures at least 44 by 44 px. The track is the R-33
drawing, not a control, so it is 10 px tall; its accessible name is
`SMAP soil moisture availability`.

## Order of the controls, and their names

```
1. Opacity, SMAP soil moisture
2. Move SMAP soil moisture up
3. Move SMAP soil moisture down
4. Pin date, SMAP soil moisture
   (the availability track: SMAP soil moisture availability)
5. Compare, SMAP soil moisture
```

That is the order R-26 fixes. `Compare, ...` appears only for a tile layer: the expanded row
of `GRACE water storage, stale` carries no compare control, and the vitest case
`offers no compare control on a layer that is not a tile layer` asserts it.

## Scope note, reported plainly

R-26's fifth item is "the availability track of that layer (R-33)". This wave renders the
track element, names it and places it in the row; the marks inside it are R-33's and are
drawn by wave 3, whose criterion is AC-27. AC-21 requires the row to carry "its track", which
it does.

Reproduce: open the app at 1440x900 and read
`document.querySelectorAll('.layer-entry')` and the boxes of the `.layer-options` controls.
