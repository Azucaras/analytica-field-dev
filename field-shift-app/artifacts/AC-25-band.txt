# AC-25 (R-31), the season band, five branches

Method: `npx vitest run` on recorded copies and on the file in the tree, plus the real app
in Chromium at 1440x900 (dev 127.0.0.1:5199, backend 127.0.0.1:8001).

The band reads `window_risk_20` from `artifacts/zarc-mt-snapshot.json` (sha256
`b53c8a7be60948fccb72e5cfa108b95debfdd932f5f15b315363d42a332dfde9`, 93 rows) and draws the
envelope of the Sequeiro rows for the field's municipality, crops and derived season only
(OD-21, OD-22).

The browser readouts above are the `data-season-window` attributes and label text of
`.timeline-season-band`; each window is labelled with its snapshot crop name, so the maize
band shows both `Milho 1ª Safra` and `Milho 2ª Safra`. No window of the other season renders
at either pinned date: the component receives one derived season and filters the rows by the
normalised key.

## (e) The recorded sha256 gate

`src/lib/season-band.test.ts` reads the file, hashes the raw bytes and compares against
`b53c8a7be60948fccb72e5cfa108b95debfdd932f5f15b315363d42a332dfde9` before it asserts any
envelope value. Measured on 2026-11-14:
```

```

season reads as a crop calendar. That is a drawing choice; the dates and the labels above
are the data facts the criterion asserts.
