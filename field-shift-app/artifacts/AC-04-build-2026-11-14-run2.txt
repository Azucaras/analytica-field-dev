Commands, literal from R-10 of the frozen spec except the Brazil detail line, which
repair card t_d7457a47 replaced with the tippecanoe command recorded here (the only
other addition is `rm -f` before a build, because tippecanoe and pmtiles refuse to
overwrite an output and R-10 names no --force flag):

tippecanoe -l trazo -Z{D} -z14 -y csbid -y csbacres -y cdl2018 -y cdl2019 -y cdl2020 -y cdl2021 -y cdl2022 -y cdl2023 -y cdl2024 -y cdl2025 -o data/polygons/us-38017-detail.pmtiles data/work/us-38017.geojson
tippecanoe -l trazo -Z{D-3} -z{D-1} -y cdl2018 -y cdl2019 -y cdl2020 -y cdl2021 -y cdl2022 -y cdl2023 -y cdl2024 -y cdl2025 --coalesce --coalesce-smallest-as-needed --maximum-tile-bytes=300000 -o data/polygons/us-38017-overview.pmtiles data/work/us-38017.geojson
pmtiles extract https://data.source.coop/wri-data-lab/trazofields/trazo3-fields/trazo3.pmtiles data/polygons/br-mt-detail.pmtiles --bbox=-61.633,-18.0416,-50.2248,-7.3487

tippecanoe -l trazo -Z8 -z12 -y Id -y mbmode24 --low-detail=10 --full-detail=12 --buffer=12 --clip-bounding-box=-61.633,-18.0416,-50.2248,-7.3487 --drop-densest-as-needed --maximum-tile-bytes=250000 -o data/polygons/br-mt-detail.pmtiles data/work/trazo3_brazil_mato_grosso_2024.fgb
tippecanoe -l trazo -Z5 -z7 -y mbmode24 --reorder --coalesce --low-detail=10 --full-detail=10 --buffer=2 --simplification=4 --maximum-tile-bytes=300000 --coalesce-smallest-as-needed -o data/polygons/br-mt-overview.pmtiles data/work/trazo3_brazil_mato_grosso_2024.fgb
