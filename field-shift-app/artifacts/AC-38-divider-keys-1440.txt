# AC-38 (R-22) compare divider keys, 1440x900, computer use

Method: the real app in Chromium at 1440x900, dev server 127.0.0.1:5199, backend
127.0.0.1:8001. Compare is on for `ndvi-8day`; `Compare divider` is focused and the keys are
dispatched through CDP `Input.dispatchKeyEvent` (trusted events). One step is 1 percent of
the map width, the design target R-22 states.

```
Focus                                               Compare divider
aria-valuetext before                               "50% of the map width"
aria-valuenow before                                50
camera centre before                                [-55.27, -11.91]
zoom before                                         11

ArrowRight                                          aria-valuenow 51
ArrowRight                                          aria-valuenow 52
ArrowLeft                                           aria-valuenow 51

focus after the keys                                Compare divider
camera centre after                                 [-55.27, -11.91]   (unchanged)
zoom after                                          11                  (unchanged)
```

Verdict: ArrowRight moves the divider one step, ArrowLeft moves it back, `aria-valuenow`
changes on every press, focus stays on the divider, and the map centre and zoom do not
change.

Reproduce: turn on `MODIS NDVI, 8 day`, press `Compare, MODIS NDVI, 8 day`, focus
`Compare divider` and press the arrow keys.
