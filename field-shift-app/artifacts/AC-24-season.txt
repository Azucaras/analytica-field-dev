# AC-24 (R-29, R-30), the derived season and the fixture label

Method: `npx vitest run` for `seasonForDate` and the panel label, plus the real app in
Chromium at 1440x900 against dev 127.0.0.1:5199 and backend 127.0.0.1:8001.

## seasonForDate, the four pinned dates (AC-24, first clause)

```
2015-04-01 -> 2014/15
2025-06-30 -> 2024/25
2025-07-01 -> 2025/26
2026-11-12 -> 2026/27
```

The rule: a season starts on 1 July, so Y is the year of the most recent 1 July on or before
the date, and the label is `Y/YY` with two digits after the slash.

scenario values and prints the label when the derived season differs.
