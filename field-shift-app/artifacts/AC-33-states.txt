Why this run replaces the first gate's AC-33 reading
--------------------------------------------------
The first gate resized with `Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride`, and that Chromium delivered
neither a `resize` event nor a media-query change to the page, so `useMediaMatch` kept the
1440 first-paint bucket at 780x900 and the `Timeline controls` toggle never mounted. Here the
window itself is resized, and the page receives both events: 2 `resize` and 3 `mq-change` entries are in the log below, in order.

What this run does not claim
---------------------------
- It does not claim the user's own display or a phone at 360: the window is a headless Chromium
  window resized through CDP, with devicePixelRatio 1.
- It does not claim a pointer or touch drag; the two presses are CDP mouse clicks at the toggle's
  own coordinates (`locator.click()`).
- It does not claim the dropdown or the picker of the native date input; the date is set through
  the input's own value event, as the first gate did.
