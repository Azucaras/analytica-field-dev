---
title: Glossary
description: What a term means here, and what it is not.
---

| Term | What it means here | Not to be confused with |
|---|---|---|
| Observed | A value a sensor measured, with acquisition time and footprint | Retrieved |
| Retrieved | A geophysical value inverted from a measurement, carrying retrieval error | Observed |
| Modelled | A value a land model produced, for example SMAP L4 root zone | Any observation |
| Entered | A fact the farmer typed, carrying an author and a date | Any satellite layer |
| Posting | The grid a product is stored on | Footprint, which is the real support |
| Safrinha | The second crop sown after soybean harvest | A rotation plan |
| ZARC | Agricultural risk zoning, municipality plus soil plus cultivar cycle | A credit product |
| PROAGRO | Crop insurance linked to rural credit enrolment | A payout guarantee |
| Abstention gate | The rule that decides show, qualify or withhold | An error message |
| Coverage | Share of the field with valid observation in the window | Confidence |
