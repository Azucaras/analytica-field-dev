## What this site is

The working documentation for a NASA Space Apps 2026 entry to the Field Shift challenge: a decision-support tool that helps a farmer weigh rotation options against soil, water and market constraints using satellite observation, with an interactive map, an agent that answers questions over the data, and voice control.

Two doors into the same content. The research pages carry the evidence, including the parts that argue against the easy version of the idea.
