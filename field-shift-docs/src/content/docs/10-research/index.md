This phase answers four questions in order. Read them in order, because each one narrows the next.

1. [Problem](/10-research/r1-problem/): who is deciding what, how many of them, and what the demo does not cover.
2. [Evidence log](/10-research/r2-evidence-log/): every check that was run, with the exact command and a confidence level.
3. [Findings](/10-research/r3-findings/): what the evidence supports, each with the thing that would overturn it.
4. [Open questions](/10-research/r4-open-questions/): what is unresolved, and who owns it.

The full cited dossier behind these pages is [here as a file](/field-shift-dossier.md). It carries 150 sources and the physical limits of every sensor the challenge names.

## Why the research comes first

The challenge invites rotation advice built on satellite data. The satellite observes proxies with hard limits, and the constraints that actually decide a rotation sit mostly outside the satellite, with landlords, lenders, co-ops and the calendar. A tool that skips this phase ships confident answers to questions the data cannot reach.
