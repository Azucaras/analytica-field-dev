Every finding names the evidence rows it rests on and the falsifier that would change it. A finding with no evidence row belongs in the design notes instead.

## F-01 The satellite can rank attention, not outcomes

SMAP reads the top 5 cm at 9 km posting from a 36 km footprint. GRACE mascons are 300 to 330 km wide and monthly. ECOSTRESS reaches 70 m but only on clear, irregular overpasses and its evapotranspiration is model-derived.

Evidence: E-05, E-06. Confidence: documented.

Falsifier: a published field-scale validation showing a 36 km soil-moisture average predicting a within-field deficit well enough to change an irrigation call.

## F-02 The Cerrado rotation question is a calendar question

The second crop has a hard upper limit near 20 February in the Mato Grosso trial setting, and soybean maturity group sets whether the window is reachable at all.

Evidence: the Santa Carmem trial and the Dois Vizinhos maturity study, both in T4. Confidence: documented.

Falsifier: a cultivar or management combination that shifts the safe second-crop window by three weeks or more.

## F-03 Local constraints bind harder than agronomy

Cash rent, crop-insurance base acres, ZARC compliance, landlord permission and co-op netback decide feasibility before a soil argument arrives.

Evidence: 21 US drivers and 27 Brazilian drivers logged. Confidence: documented.

Falsifier: a region where rent is negligible and credit does not depend on zoning compliance.

## F-04 No Brazilian soil layer matches SSURGO grade

Brazil has no nationwide survey at US map-unit quality; coverage at 1:100,000 or finer is under 5 percent.

Evidence: E-08. Confidence: documented.

Falsifier: a released, licensed national soil raster at field scale.

## F-05 The Atlas companion site already encodes a build sequence

It ranks six datasets, supplies a first-hour recipe and ships starter code that runs, and it has no scoring or submission flow.

Evidence: E-03, E-09. Confidence: measured.

Falsifier: a scoring surface appearing in the expected 2026-11-13 resource drop.

## F-06 The unoccupied gap is correction, not access

Agreena already advertises free entry plus satellite crop, tillage and cover-crop recognition, and FieldView has a free tier. What none of them show is a farmer-correctable, provenance-transparent, multi-objective comparison.

Evidence: prior-art pack, section 7. Confidence: documented.

Falsifier: a shipping product that exposes per-claim provenance and farmer correction over its own history.
