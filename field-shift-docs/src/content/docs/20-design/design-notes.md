## Why the card, not the map, carries the answer

A map shows where. It cannot show why a sequence was suggested or who has to confirm it. The card is the unit that holds the five challenge fields, and the map is the way to reach the card. This is an argument, not a finding: no usability test has been run.

## Why the abstention gate is provisional

Eighty percent valid area and two supported seasons is a round number chosen to be defensible rather than measured. It exists so the interface has one rule instead of a judgement call per field. Expect to move it once three or four real fields have been tried.

## What a good failure looks like

The tool should fail loudly and early: a field with one season of evidence and 40 percent cloud returns no ranking, says which of the two thresholds failed, and links the missing input. A silent partial answer is the failure mode worth designing against.

## Terms that keep causing arguments

See the [glossary](/40-reference/glossary/). Observed, retrieved, modelled and entered are four different things and the whole honesty story collapses if they are used interchangeably.
